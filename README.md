💼 Job & Internship Portal

A modern and responsive Job & Internship Portal designed to help students, freshers, and job seekers discover relevant job and internship opportunities in one place.

🌐 Live Demo

🔗 "View Live Website" (https://job-internship-portal-iqw7ckgmn-sahedashaik9-7845s-projects.vercel.app)

📌 Overview

The Job & Internship Portal provides a simple and user-friendly platform where users can explore available job and internship opportunities, view important details, and navigate to application pages.

The project was developed to provide a practical solution for students and freshers who are looking for career opportunities.

✨ Features

- 🔍 Browse job and internship opportunities
- 💼 Separate job and internship sections
- 🔎 Search and explore opportunities
- 🏢 Display company and job details
- 📍 Location and role information
- 🔗 Application links
- 📱 Fully responsive design
- 🎨 Clean and modern user interface
- ⚡ Simple and easy navigation

🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5 – Website structure
- CSS3 – Styling and responsive design
- JavaScript – Interactivity and dynamic functionality
